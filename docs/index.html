COS. Calculation of SalaryAll amounts are monthly. Your estimate uses the salary, taxes, expenses, and savings you enter.

Your monthly budget, explained.

Enter a monthly salary, your regular expenses, and the amount you want to save.

Before tax: salary − taxes = take-home pay.

After tax: your salary is already take-home pay. Taxes are not deducted again.

Remaining: take-home pay − expenses − savings.

Taxes can be a monthly amount or a percentage of gross salary. Use your own figure; COS does not apply country-specific tax rules. A negative balance is a shortfall. Annual savings are your monthly goal × 12, assuming you meet that goal each month.

The example is illustrative. All amounts use the selected currency; changing currency does not convert values. Your entries stay in this tab and are cleared when the page is reloaded.
